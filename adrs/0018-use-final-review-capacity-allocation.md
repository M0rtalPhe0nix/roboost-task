# Use the final review-capacity allocation

The initial 5% human-review capacity for Product Listing Intelligence is allocated 70% to
prohibited gray-zone and detector-disagreement cases, 20% to adversarial, inconsistent, or
suspected-counterfeit cases, 1% to representative unknown-category and emerging-product cases, and
9% to stratified audit of auto-approved listings. The allocation may adapt to observed queue volume,
and emergency safety signals may preempt it. This replaces the earlier 80/15/5 proposal with a more
explicit balance between safety ambiguity, adversarial behavior, taxonomy drift, and false-negative
audit coverage.
