# Show evidence strength for operational calculations

The Pharmacy Operations Assistant shows data-point counts and a high, medium, or low Evidence Strength label for material calculations, based on sample size, time coverage, and completeness. It suppresses branch rankings below a minimum comparable-data floor so sparse or newly observed branches are not presented as reliable performance findings.

The assistant also declines Unsupported Questions and names the missing data source rather than inferring an answer from incomplete operational evidence.
