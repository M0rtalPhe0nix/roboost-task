# Separate intent from urgency in message triage

Each Customer Message will retain an Intent and independent Urgency signal, then produce the assessment-required single Triage Label with urgent escalation taking priority. Urgency covers explicit legal, regulatory, or social-media escalation threats and credible safety, health, or personal-data harm; ordinary anger and churn intent do not qualify. This prevents an urgent refund or complaint from losing its business context while still giving operations one unambiguous routing outcome.
